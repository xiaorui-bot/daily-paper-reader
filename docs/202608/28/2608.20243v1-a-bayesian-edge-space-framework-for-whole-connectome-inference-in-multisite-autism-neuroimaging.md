## 摘要
自闭症谱系障碍（ASD）与跨分布式脑系统的异质性改变相关，这对全连接组推断构成了挑战。困难不仅源于连接数量庞大，还源于由解剖学和功能上相关的脑区配对所索引的效应之间的依赖性。我们引入了一种贝叶斯边空间回归框架，将每个参与者的连接组视为网络值响应，并对无序脑区配对上的调整后ASD效应进行建模。主要的方法论贡献是直接在连接上定义半正定协方差构造。通过一种对称化端点匹配操作，将解剖学和诊断盲的功能相似性从脑区提升到边空间，该操作保留端点身份且对端点顺序不变。一个加性贝叶斯层次结构估计了解剖学、功能性和交互贡献，以及多中心调整和连接特异性效应。理论结果确立了协方差有效性和结构化组件的连续嵌套性。低秩核表示和精确的充分统计量约简使得全连接组计算无需初步的逐边估计。模拟显示效应表面的恢复有所改进，特别是在弱信号下。在自闭症脑成像数据交换中，该框架识别出广泛的连接减少以及ASD相关连接性的局部增加。这种模式支持跨分布式神经系统的异质性重组，而非均匀的高连通性或低连通性。在拟合的参数化下，功能性组件具有最大的结构尺度，表明组织超越了单纯的解剖邻近性。

## Abstract
Autism spectrum disorder (ASD) is associated with heterogeneous alterations across distributed brain systems, creating challenges for whole-connectome inference. The difficulty arises not only from the large number of connections, but also from dependence among effects indexed by anatomically and functionally related region pairs. We introduce a Bayesian Edge-Space regression framework that treats each participant's connectome as a network-valued response and models the adjusted ASD effect over unordered brain-region pairs. The main methodological contribution is a positive-semidefinite covariance construction defined directly on connections. Anatomical and diagnosis-blind functional similarities are lifted from regions to edge space through a symmetrized endpoint-matching operation that preserves endpoint identity and is invariant to endpoint ordering. An additive Bayesian hierarchy estimates anatomical, functional, and interaction contributions together with multisite adjustments and connection-specific effects. Theoretical results establish covariance validity and continuous nesting of the structured components. Low-rank kernel representations and an exact sufficient-statistic reduction enable whole-connectome computation without preliminary edgewise estimation. Simulations show improved recovery of the effect surface, particularly under weak signals. In the Autism Brain Imaging Data Exchange, the framework identifies widespread reductions together with localized increases in ASD-associated connectivity. This pattern supports heterogeneous reorganization across distributed neural systems rather than uniform hyper- or hypoconnectivity. Under the fitted parameterization, the functional component has the largest structural scale, indicating organization beyond anatomical proximity alone.

## 摘要
当模型包含许多相关参数时，贝叶斯变量选择在计算上变得具有挑战性。我们研究分段确定性马尔可夫过程（PDMP）采样器，作为传统马尔可夫链蒙特卡罗在尖峰-平板变量选择中的连续时间替代方案。在粘性PDMP采样器中，活跃参数连续演化直至达到零，它们可能在零处停留随机时长，然后重新进入模型。当一个参数进入或离开模型时，其余参数继续沿确定性流演化，为探索具有强相关参数的后验分布提供了一种潜在有利的机制。我们做出两项方法论贡献。首先，我们将现有粘性PDMP方法从独立尖峰-平板先验扩展到相关模型先验和相关平板分布。其次，我们研究了当似然分解为多个因子时，使用无偏随机梯度来降低变量选择计算成本，同时保持相同的目标分布。我们研究这两种扩展应用于两个心理测量模型：高斯随机截距交叉滞后面板模型和序数马尔可夫随机场。对于前者，边缘化产生固定维度的充分统计量表示，从而允许高效的模型评估。对于后者，模型可分解，从而能够对个人节点贡献进行子采样。在模拟研究中，我们将ZigZag、Bouncy Particle和Boomerang动力学与可逆跳跃MCMC进行比较，并检验先验相关性和随机梯度子采样对采样效率的影响。我们使用一项关于心理健康的实证研究数据来说明该方法。最后，我们讨论了使用PDMP采样器进行贝叶斯变量选择的优势与挑战。

## Abstract
Bayesian variable selection becomes computationally challenging when models contain many dependent parameters. We study Piecewise Deterministic Markov Process (PDMP) samplers as a continuous-time alternative to conventional Markov chain Monte Carlo for spike-and-slab variable selection. In sticky PDMP samplers, active parameters evolve continuously until they reach zero, where they may remain for a random duration before re-entering the model. While one parameter enters or leaves the model, the remaining parameters continue to evolve along the deterministic flow, offering a potentially advantageous mechanism for exploring posteriors with strongly dependent parameters. We make two methodological contributions. First, we extend existing sticky PDMP methods beyond independent spike-and-slab priors to dependent model priors and dependent slab distributions. Second, we investigate the use of unbiased stochastic gradients to reduce the computational cost of variable selection when the likelihood decomposes into many factors while retaining the same target distribution. We study these extensions to two psychometric models: a Gaussian random intercept cross-lagged panel model and an ordinal Markov random field. For the former, marginalization yields a fixed-dimensional sufficient-statistic representation that permits efficient model evaluation. For the latter, the model factorizes, which enables subsampling over person-node contributions. In simulation studies, we compare ZigZag, Bouncy Particle, and Boomerang dynamics with reversible-jump MCMC, and examine the effects of prior dependence and stochastic-gradient subsampling on sampling efficiency. We illustrate the methodology using data from an empirical study on mental well-being. Finally, we discuss the advantages and challenges when using PDMP samplers for Bayesian variable selection.
